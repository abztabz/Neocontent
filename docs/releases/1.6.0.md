# NeoContent 1.6.0

This coordinated cloud and WordPress release responds to the first customer content and workflow review.

## Customer experience

- Restores a permanent **Settings** tab after activation.
- Saves profile, tone, service, location, content-mode and cadence changes locally, then synchronizes them safely after the admin page renders.
- Converts delivered article HTML into native Gutenberg paragraph, heading, list, quote, image and separator blocks.
- Adds a customer-visible visual brief containing featured/banner and inline-image placement suggestions.
- Shows the next scheduled research time and cadence on the Researching screen.

## Research and editorial quality

- Generates multiple headline options and a clearer why-now rationale.
- Adds timely industry opportunities alongside durable service and customer-demand topics.
- Passes primary and supporting keyword hypotheses, search intent, timeliness and recommended publication date into the governed Luna brief.
- Explicitly prevents unverified keyword estimates from being represented as measured search volume, traffic, difficulty or rankings.
- Requires semantic article structure and generates a safe image plan without invented image URLs or licensing claims.

## Continuous free workflow

- Adds Vercel-scheduled research for operator-managed sites using the customer's saved cadence.
- Creates only one active article per customer at a time.
- Schedules the next research cycle after approval or rejection.
- Uses cadence-bound idempotency keys so retries cannot create duplicate briefs.
- Keeps all recurring work out of customer WordPress hosts; no NeoContent WordPress cron or polling is introduced.
